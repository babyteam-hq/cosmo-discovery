# Cosmo sweep 4: candidates from recent papers' own open follow-ups (2026-10-31, cloud worker cosmo-sweep-6, R-120)

Why this sweep is different: the three earlier sweeps (`sweep.md`, `sweep-2.md`, `sweep-3.md`, ~90 tests) brainstormed tests
on public catalogues, and the literature check or the gate killed most. Here the candidate source is the literature itself:
astro-ph.CO / GA / HE papers submitted 2026-04 to 2026-10 (arXiv export API, 555 abstracts pooled by topic queries; 73
selected for full-text reading by six reading agents, discussion and conclusions read in the HTML or PDF, not the abstract),
each yielding zero or more lines of the form "they say it is not done, and it could be done with public data X". Parked
threads were excluded at harvest time (SN residuals vs dust maps; CF4 zero points and bulk flows; NGC 1052 trail; eRASS1
anisotropy; Pantheon+ sky anisotropy; count dipoles). Every quoted sentence is under 15 words and verbatim from the paper.

Ranking: detectability × cheapness, with public data reachable from this network (checked: arXiv, data.desi.lbl.gov,
IRSA TAP, vizier.idia.ac.za, gaia.aip.de TAP, assets.chime-frb.ca, GitHub raw, data.sdss.org, PLA, ztfcosmo.in2p3.fr,
projets.ip2i.in2p3.fr, heasarc; not: zenodo.org, LAMBDA data dirs, GitHub release assets).

## Harvest

75 papers read in full (six reading agents, 12-13 each; the lists are in each group header's table); 95 lines harvested. The agents' tables are kept as written (value 1-5 is the agent's), grouped by theme.
