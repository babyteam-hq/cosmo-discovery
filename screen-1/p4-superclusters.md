# Proposed test: the mass of eRASS1 superclusters outside their member clusters, from stacked ACT DR6 CMB lensing

Question: what fraction of a supercluster's mass lies outside its member clusters? Stack the ACT DR6 CMB-lensing convergence on the eRASS1 supercluster catalogue (Liu et al. 2024, built from eRASS1 clusters whose masses are weak-lensing calibrated) and compare the total projected lensing mass with the sum of the member clusters' catalogue masses: the excess, as a fraction with its interval.

Data: eRASS1 supercluster catalogue (Liu et al. 2024) and the eRASS1 cluster catalogue with WL-calibrated masses (Bulbul et al. 2024; Ghirardini et al. 2024); ACT DR6 lensing convergence with its 400 released simulations; Planck PR4 lensing as a check.

Method: superclusters with at least 3 members at 0.05 < z < 0.6; for each, a model map of its member clusters (NFW at catalogue mass, convolved with the beam); the statistic is the stacked kappa in the supercluster's footprint minus the member model, normalised by the member mass. Samples, apertures and the member-mass model fixed before any stack. Covariance from the 400 simulations; random and rotated nulls; the same statistic on random cluster groupings of matched member count as the two-halo control.

Expected precision: about 1,000 superclusters in the ACT footprint; total kappa detected at > 15 sigma; the outside-cluster fraction to about 0.05-0.1 absolute.
