# Proposed test: the CMB-lensing signal of filaments between eRASS1 cluster pairs, at ACT DR6 resolution

Question: is there excess lensing convergence along the axis between pairs of X-ray clusters (eRASS1) after subtracting the clusters' own profiles, and what is its amplitude relative to the LCDM expectation for bridges between halos of those (weak-lensing-calibrated) masses?

Data: eRASS1 cluster catalogue with WL-calibrated masses; ACT DR6 lensing convergence and its 400 simulations; Planck PR4 lensing as a check.

Method: cluster pairs with 3D separation 5-15 Mpc from catalogue redshifts and both masses above 10^14; rotate and rescale each pair to a common frame; subtract the two clusters' model profiles (NFW at catalogue masses, beam-convolved); the statistic is the mean kappa in a fixed rectangle between them, compared with the same stack on non-physical (projected but redshift-separated) pairs as the control. All fixed before stacking. Covariance from the 400 simulations; rotated-map nulls.

Expected precision: about 3,000-6,000 physical pairs in the ACT footprint; filament kappa at 3-5 sigma; amplitude to 25-30 %.
