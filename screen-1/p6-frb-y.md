# Proposed test: the hot-gas pressure along fast-radio-burst sightlines, from ACT DR6 Compton-y at the positions of CHIME FRBs

Question: do FRBs with excess dispersion measure (DM beyond the Milky Way and the mean cosmic Macquart relation at their redshift proxy) sit behind more thermal-SZ pressure? The cross-correlation of FRB DM excess with ACT DR6 and Planck Compton-y separates the DM contributed by hot (virialised, intra-group) gas from the cool diffuse IGM and the host.

Data: CHIME/FRB Catalog 2 (positions with their localisation regions, DMs, scattering), with host redshifts for the localised subset (CHIME Outriggers, DSA-110, ASKAP CRAFT); ACT DR6 Compton-y maps (with the CIB-deprojected variant) and Planck MILCA y; YMW16 and NE2001 Galactic DM models.

Method: fixed before any stack: the FRB sample (|b| > 20 deg, outside the y-map masks), DM-excess bins (terciles), the aperture (the CHIME localisation convolved with the y beam). Statistic: the mean y in each DM-excess bin minus the mean over random positions drawn from CHIME's own sky-exposure selection; the slope dy/dDM with its interval. Errors from the y-map noise realisations and random-position stacks; nulls from DM-shuffled samples. Interpret through a halo model of DM and y from the same gas.

Expected precision: about 3,000 FRBs in the overlap; dy/dDM detected at 2-4 sigma if intervening groups contribute ~20 % of the DM excess; an upper limit otherwise.
