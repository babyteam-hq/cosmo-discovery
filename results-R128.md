# Results (R-128, cosmo-deep-8, 2026-11-01)

Lensing magnification as an instrument for the JWST z > 8 bright-end and mass excess: do the de-magnified properties of z > 8
galaxies behind Abell 2744 depend on the magnification beyond what the selection predicts? Every number from `jwst_mu_test.py`
(usage in its docstring); data under `data/` (gitignored; `data/dja/fetch_dja.sh`, `data/uncover/fetch_uncover.sh`,
`data/canucs/fetch_canucs.sh` re-fetch it). Logs in `logs/`. This file is the worker's own.

## The two flagged papers, read in full before computing

- **arXiv:2512.00730** (Xuheng Ma, sole author; v2 2026-08-29, "A source-plane reassessment of the z = 6-8 ultraviolet luminosity
  function behind Abell 2744: no requirement for additional faint-end curvature"). Uses only the UNCOVER v2.0 lens model (Furtak &
  Zitrin; Price et al. 2025) and propagates its 50 released MCMC realizations into the UV LF's Schechter parameters and ΔAIC, calling
  that "within-family lens sensitivity, not ... a comparison among independent reconstructions". It does **not** tabulate per-source
  magnification errors, does **not** regress or test any de-magnified property (M_UV, mass, size, SFR) against mu (mu appears only as
  the colour of a z-M1500 scatter plot), compares with blank fields only qualitatively (Bouwens+2021, Finkelstein+2015, Atek+2018
  overplotted on the z = 6-8 LF; "contextual, not a joint likelihood"), and never mentions z > 8 or the bright/massive excess
  ("the total UV-selected population at 6 ≤ z < 8, rather than an emission-line or z > 9 subsample"). Sample: DR3 photometry, EAZY
  P(6 ≤ z < 8) ≥ 0.6, 161 sources, 0 < mu ≤ 30.
- **arXiv:2610.32307** (Bangzheng Sun & Haojing Yan, 2026-10-23, "Galaxy Luminosity Functions at z ~ 7-10 from Four Years of JWST
  Observations"). Nine fields (seven blank: PRIMER COSMOS and UDS, EGS, HUDF-par2, GOODS-S, GOODS-N, NEXUS-Wide; two clusters: Abell
  2744 and Abell 370), F090W and F115W dropouts (z ~ 7.4 and z ~ 10), ~1,045 arcmin². One best-fit lens model per cluster (UNCOVER
  DR4 for A2744, Sharon HFF v4 for A370), **no** ensemble or lens-model error, **no** magnification cut, Poisson errors only. The LF
  is one inverse-variance-weighted average over the fields: **no** lensed-vs-blank split, ratio or tension test (nearest sentence:
  the −21.78 and −21.28 bins "contain 83 and 123 candidates selected from seven fields"), **no** property-vs-mu test, no masses or
  sizes. Bright end at z ~ 10: a power law (β = −2.36 ± 0.16), "driven by the two brightest correctable bins" with N_spec = 3 and 6.

Both leave open exactly the trajectory check's open part: the regression of de-magnified M_UV, mass and size on log mu with the
lens-model spread, the test of whether the bright and massive z > 9 sources sit at a different mu than the parent sample at the
same observed magnitude, and a formal lensed-vs-blank bright-end comparison.

## Fixed before any fit (2026-11-01 06:25 UTC, after the catalogue column inspection and the sample count, before any regression)

**Data.** Cluster arm: Abell 2744, UNCOVER DR3 SUPER photometric catalogue (Weaver et al. 2024, 74,020 objects, EAZY photo-z with
P(z) percentiles z025/z160/z500/z840/z975, F444W-matched, bCG-subtracted) with the DR4 v2.0 magnifications of the same rows (Furtak &
Zitrin model, Price et al. 2025: best-fit mu and the 68/95 % posterior percentiles from the MCMC chain, at the EAZY photo-z) and
the DR4 Prospector-β SPS catalogue of the same rows (Wang et al. 2024; masses de-magnified on the fly with the v2.0 model). The
DR3 catalogue also carries the v1.1 model's mu for the same objects (median log ratio v2.0/v1.1 0.016, scatter 0.10 dex on the
z > 8 sample): a second version of the family, used as a check. Blank arm (and a pipeline-consistent cluster arm): the DJA grizli
v7 photometric catalogues with their eazy-py photo-z (`*-fix_phot_apcorr.fits`, `*.eazypy.zout.fits`) for abell2744clu v7.2,
abell2744par v7.0, ceers-full v7.4, gds v7.2, gdn v7.3, primer-uds-north and -south v7.2, ngdeep v7.2. Further cluster fields if
time allows: CANUCS DR1 (A370, MACS0416, MACS1149; Dense Basis photo-z catalogues, Lenstool best-fit kappa and gamma maps and 100
MCMC sample maps, all from MAST). zenodo and GitHub releases are 403 from this cloud; archive.stsci.edu times out but the
mast.stsci.edu download API and astroquery work; the UNCOVER release is on Google Drive, which answers.

**Selection (one rule, every field).** use_phot = 1 (or the DJA equivalent: not a star, not flagged), flag_eazy = 1 where it exists,
z_phot > 8 and z160 > 7 (the 16th percentile of P(z) above 7), signal-to-noise ≥ 5 in the rest-UV band (F150W for z_phot < 10.5,
F200W above; a variant at ≥ 3 is reported), and for the cluster arm 1.2 ≤ mu ≤ 30 (the critical-curve neighbourhood excluded:
posteriors there are multimodal and the sample would be dominated by the lens model) and no object within 0.5" of a multiple
image in the v2.0 constraint catalogue. The count after these cuts on A2744 (UNCOVER): 105 at S/N ≥ 5 (215 at ≥ 3), before the mu
and multiple-image cuts.

**Quantities.** M_UV = m_UV − DM(z_phot) + 2.5 log10(1 + z_phot) + 2.5 log10 mu (flat f_nu K-correction; Planck 2018 flat ΛCDM, H0 =
67.7, Ωm = 0.31). log M* = mstar_50 of the SPS catalogue (already de-magnified). Size: r = sqrt(max(flux_radius² − r_psf², 0) / mu)
with r_psf the half-light radius of the detection-image PSF (F444W-matched, 0.08"); reported with its PSF floor as a secondary
statistic because the z > 8 sample is mostly unresolved.

**Statistics (the claim is these numbers with their intervals).**
- R1: ordinary least squares of M_UV on log10 mu; slope and 68/95 % intervals.
- R2: least squares of log M* on (M_UV, log10 mu) jointly; the log10 mu coefficient.
- R3: least squares of log r on (M_UV, log10 mu) jointly; the log10 mu coefficient.
- T4: for the bright (M_UV < −20) and massive (log M* > 9.5) z_phot > 9 sources, the shift of their median log10 mu from the
  parent sample's at the same observed m_UV (stratified in 0.5-mag bins; a stratified permutation test, 10,000 permutations).
- C5: lensed vs blank: the number of z_phot > 8 sources brighter than M_UV = −20 (and −20.5) per source-plane comoving volume
  (8 < z < 10 and 10 < z < 13 in the blank fields as well), A2744 against the blank fields together, as a ratio with its interval.
  Same DJA eazy selection in every field; mu for the DJA A2744 objects from the UNCOVER DR4 catalogue matched within 0.3".
  Source-plane area: the image-plane footprint in 6" cells holding at least one use_phot object, each cell divided by the v2.0 mu
  at z = 9 (the mean of the z = 8 and z = 10 maps) at its centre; blank fields the cell area. M_UV < −20 is m_UV ≈ 27.3 at z = 9,
  more than a magnitude above every field's 5σ depth, so no completeness correction is applied at the bright cut (the depth maps
  are checked and the shallowest cells dropped if needed, before the count).
- Errors on everything: 1,000 bootstraps over objects; 200 draws of mu from a split-normal in log mu fitted to the 16th/84th
  posterior percentiles (the lens-model spread); 200 draws of z from z160/z500/z840 (photo-z); a jackknife over four sky quadrants
  of the cluster field (and over fields where more than one cluster enters); the v1.1 model substituted for v2.0 as a check. For
  C5, Poisson plus cosmic variance from linear theory (σ² = b² σ²_DM(V), b = 8 for M_UV < −20 at z ~ 9, stated as approximate).
- The expected slopes under a correct lens model and no contamination (the forward model F): sources drawn from a z ~ 9 UV LF
  (Donnan et al. 2024 double power law; Bouwens et al. 2021 Schechter as a bracket) placed uniformly in the source plane (image-plane
  density ∝ 1/mu), magnified by the v2.0 z = 9 map, detected with the catalogue's own local depth (the median rest-UV flux error of
  the catalogued objects in the cell, 5σ, a 0.3-mag soft edge) and the same S/N cut; a mass-M_UV relation log M* = 9.0 − 0.4
  (M_UV + 20) with 0.4 dex scatter and sizes from r_e = 0.6 kpc (L/L*)^0.3 with 0.2 dex scatter and the PSF floor, detection in the
  LW stack from the same model; the same estimators R1-R3 and T4 run on 1,000 mocks of the data's size give the expected slope and
  its scatter. The data's slope is compared with the mock distribution; the reported quantity is the data slope minus the expected
  slope with the combined interval.
- Trials: the rows below count every statistic computed; nothing is added after the data are seen except as a labelled check.
