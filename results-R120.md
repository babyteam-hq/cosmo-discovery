Referees' design points carried into the rows: (A) Ravi+ already used one burst per source (3,524 unique sources, 2,812 after cuts, NE2025), so only the S/N-regressed remap and the 2MRS part are new; (B1) half of ZTF DR2's redshifts come from archival SDSS host spectra, so the random catalogue must follow the SN sky distribution, not just n(z), and the x1 split will be weaker than forecast; (C) DESI/SDSS footprints are partial sky, so glide and spin mix and per-bin errors exceed the all-sky forecast, and spin is known to depend on G, which the redshift test must control; (D) low-x1 SNe are fainter, so the full-sample bias corrections do not hold for the halves at high z (SNLS3 cut in redshift for this reason).

Not run, decided here: B2 (SN cousins) because the referee left open only a 1-2σ question (the excess beyond the shared-
peculiar-velocity reduction) on a sample that overlaps TITAN's; D (x1-split cosmology) because its open part is confounded by
the full-sample bias corrections at high z (the referee's point, SNLS3's reason for a redshift cut) and a cheap Pantheon+-only
version would be a consistency check, not a measurement; both go back to the pool with their verdicts on file.
