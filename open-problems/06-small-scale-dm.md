Mapped 2026-11-01. The scan for open problems used one extended WebSearch, then the full text of the
newest field review, Nadler, Rogers & Drlica-Wagner, "Dark Matter Constraints from Small-Scale Cosmic
Structure" (arXiv:2607.28564, July 2026), which covers dwarfs, lensing, Ly-a, streams, weak lensing,
21cm and simulations. Every claim below was then checked against arXiv abstract pages.

## What survives

- **Gaia DR3 astrometric-lensing subhalo limit** (2002.01938 update). Highest readiness. Openness rests on an
  unrun search for "astrometric weak lensing Gaia DR3 subhalo".
- **Selection-forward orbital-phase test of MW satellites** (1805.00908). Openness also rests on unrun searches
  ("satellite orbital phase pericentre Gaia selection function", "first infall satellites orbital phase").
- The LMC-wake amplitude and the SAGA plane-incidence rows are probably partly done. Search before any work.
- The velocity-dispersion function is stated open by the 2026 review, but its spectroscopic selection is the
  blocker.

Not ours or done: density-pericentre, GD-1 perturber, SDSSJ0946, GGSL, Ly-a 5 sigma, halo shape, sim stream
pericentres, satellite radial profiles, rotation-curve diversity, wide binaries.
