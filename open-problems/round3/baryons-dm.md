## What this round suggests

- Public DR1 spectroscopy is too sparse for halo-scale absorption or lensing pairs at z ~ 1. R-150's counts are about
  10^3 pairs inside 130 kpc per tracer bin. Any galaxy-selected CGM stack at z ~ 1 should start from those counts.
- eRASS1 has about a quarter of eRASS:4's exposure, which leaves diffuse CGM work S/N-starved. And LoTSS DR2 and eRASS1
  barely overlap.
- No disagreement in these two areas met all three checks. The live disputes either need data that isn't public
  (eRASS:4, DESI DR2, UNIONS shear), or come down to modelling choices that new data doesn't remove.
