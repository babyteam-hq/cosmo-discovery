- **Row 3**: EP-violation β from the DESI DR1 multi-tracer squeezed bispectrum. This is the strongest survivor.
- **Row 2**: EB-only vs TT-only lensing amplitude ratio on ACT DR6 maps. Partly done; pipeline-heavy.
- **Row 6**: CMB-SPA N_eff with an LBT Y_p prior. Incremental.
- **Row 7**: DESI Y_p. Data-limited.
- **Row 11**: open but low payoff.
