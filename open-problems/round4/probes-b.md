**Survivors: none.** Every candidate below failed one of the three checks. They're recorded so later rounds skip them.

## Data-landscape notes (new since round 3)

- **eROSITA-DE DR2 is public (2026-07-31; arXiv:2607.27772).** It has cumulative eRASS:3 catalogues for the western
  Galactic hemisphere: ~1.9M point and ~64k extended sources, LS10/Gaia/CatWISE counterparts and HEALPix 3σ
  upper-limit maps. **It is catalogue-only, with no event lists or images.** So the S/N-starved diffuse eRASS1 stacks
  from round 3 (the MW-mass hot CGM split by DESI isolation, the hardness tests) still can't be done on public data.
- **SDSS DR20 is public (2026-07-30).** It has the first southern SDSS-V BOSS spectra and is coordinated with eROSITA
  DR2.
- **Euclid DR1 is not public.** The 21 Oct 2026 date was revised: a first tranche in November 2026, the full release
  in mid-2027.

## What this round suggests

- CHIME Cat 2 FRB-baryon stacks are now covered end to end: 2604.22105's ten-tracer anthology, 2604.17162,
  2608.09014 and 2506.08932. Drop FRB DM from these areas until Cat 3 or many more localized FRBs are released.
- In 2026, DESI's own kSZ series (DR2 x ACT DR6) and the eRASS1 group papers took the obvious cluster and
  group-baryon extensions.
- The public data new since round 3 (eROSITA DR2, SDSS DR20) are catalogues, not photons. They help source-level work
  (counts, cross-matches, clustering) but not diffuse stacks. Euclid DR1 is the next big regime-opener for small-scale
  DM (strong-lens samples) and high z, and it isn't public yet. Re-screen these areas after its first tranche.
