# Proposed test: is the early-/late-type difference in the weak-lensing radial acceleration relation an artifact of photometric-redshift isolation?

Question: KiDS-1000 weak-lensing measurements of the radial acceleration relation for isolated galaxies disagree: one analysis finds early- and late-type galaxies at least 6 sigma apart, another, with a stricter isolation cut, finds one relation. Both select "isolated" lenses with photometric redshifts. Using spectroscopic redshifts, measure how often photometric-redshift-isolated lenses actually have a massive neighbour, separately for quiescent and star-forming galaxies at fixed stellar mass, and turn that type-dependent contamination into the bias it induces in the stacked excess surface density, and so in g_obs, for each type. The claim: the predicted spurious ETG/LTG offset in log g_obs with its interval, against the published offset.

Data: DESI DR1 Bright Galaxy Survey spectroscopic galaxies and the DESI DR1 BGS group catalogue (value-added, with group halo masses); the KiDS-bright photometric-redshift error model (scatter and outlier rate as published) to emulate the photometric isolation; public mock catalogues with DESI-like selection (or the group catalogue's halo masses) for the excess-surface-density prediction.

Method: fixed before computing: the lens sample (0.05 < z < 0.3, stellar-mass bins matching the KiDS analysis), the type split (colour and morphology as in the KiDS papers), the two published isolation criteria applied with emulated photometric redshifts (several random seeds), the contamination statistic (fraction with a neighbour above 10 % of the lens stellar mass within the isolation radius and 500 km/s, from spectroscopic redshifts), and the excess-surface-density bias model (the contaminating neighbour's halo, from the group catalogue's halo mass or from mocks, added to the lens's own profile, then converted to g_obs by the same deprojection the KiDS papers used). Interval from seeds, bootstrap over lenses and the halo-mass uncertainty; null: the same pipeline with the true (spectroscopic) isolation gives zero bias by construction.

Expected precision: about 60,000 lenses; contamination fractions to about 1 %; the predicted spurious offset to a few hundredths of a dex, against the published 0.14-0.19 dex.
