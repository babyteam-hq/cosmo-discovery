# Proposed test: is the CMB "extra lensing" really lensing? The EB-only to TT-only lensing-amplitude ratio on ACT DR6 maps

Question: Planck and ACT see more lensing smoothing than LCDM predicts in some analyses; a light-field modulation of the CMB (a non-lensing effect) would bias the temperature-only (TT) lensing reconstruction but not the polarisation-only EB reconstruction. Measure the ratio of the lensing amplitudes reconstructed from EB alone and from TT alone on the same ACT DR6 sky, with an interval; LCDM lensing predicts 1.

Data: the public ACT DR6 CMB maps (temperature and polarisation, the coadds used for the lensing release), the released lensing simulations and noise realisations, the ACT DR6 lensing release's own TT-only and polarisation-only products where released.

Method: fixed before reconstructing: the multipole ranges, the masks, the quadratic estimators (standard TT and EB with the released normalisations), the bias subtractions (realisation-dependent N0, N1 from simulations, mean-field from simulations), the amplitude fit over a fixed lensing multipole range. The ratio and its interval from the joint distribution over simulations (which carry the TT-EB correlation of noise and lensing). Nulls: curl reconstructions, map-split differences.

Expected precision: the EB amplitude to about 10-15 % on ACT DR6 at the noise level of the release, the ratio to about 15 %.
