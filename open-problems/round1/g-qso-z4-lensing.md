# Proposed test: the clustering bias of quasars at z ~ 3.5-5 from CMB lensing

Question: how strongly clustered are the most luminous quasars at z ~ 4? Clustering measurements at these redshifts (from the quasar auto-correlation) give very high biases that imply long quasar duty cycles and massive hosts, but they are noisy and depend on the redshift distribution. The cross-correlation with CMB lensing measures the bias directly. Measure b(z ~ 4) with an interval.

Data: SDSS DR16Q and DESI DR1 quasars at 3.5 < z < 5 (spectroscopic), their random catalogues and selection masks; ACT DR6 lensing (with its 400 simulations) and Planck PR4 lensing.

Method: fixed before computing: the redshift cuts, the magnitude limits and masks, the multipole range, the estimator (pseudo-C_ell of the quasar overdensity with kappa, with magnification bias from the measured luminosity-function slope modelled), and the fit (linear bias with the spectroscopic redshift distribution). Covariance from the lensing simulations; nulls from the lensing curl mode, randoms, and the ACT-Planck comparison.

Expected precision: about 10^4 quasars at these redshifts; the cross-power at 3-5 sigma; b to about 20-30 %.
