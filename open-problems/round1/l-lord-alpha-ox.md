# Proposed test: are low-redshift little-red-dot analogues X-ray weak? Stacked alpha_ox of SDSS V-shaped LoRDs against matched quasars

Question: JWST's little red dots are strikingly X-ray weak; whether that is intrinsic or due to Compton-thick absorption is open. Do their low-redshift analogues (the ~244 SDSS V-shaped Local Red Dots, arXiv:2606.26098) share it? Measure the mean Delta alpha_ox (offset from the alpha_ox-L_2500 relation) of the LoRDs against luminosity- and redshift-matched DR16Q quasars, with an interval, and the stacked hardness ratio.

Data: the published SDSS LoRD sample; SDSS DR16Q controls; eRASS1 0.2-2.3 keV images and the eRASS1 catalogue (stacked at the positions), 4XMM-DR14 fluxes and upper limits where covered; L_2500 from the SDSS spectra.

Method: fixed before stacking: the sample, the control matching, the stacking (eRASS1 count stacks with matched-background apertures, converted with a stated spectral model), the alpha_ox relation (Lusso & Risaliti), the hardness ratio. Interval by bootstrap over objects; nulls from stacks at random positions and on non-AGN galaxies matched in optical flux.

Expected precision: ~240 objects; Delta alpha_ox to about +-0.05-0.1, enough to test X-ray weakness at the LRD level (Delta alpha_ox < -0.3).
