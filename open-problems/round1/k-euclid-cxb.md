# Proposed test: coherence of the source-subtracted near-infrared background with the soft X-ray background, Euclid Q1 against eRASS1

Question: near-infrared background fluctuations (Spitzer, AKARI) were found coherent with the soft X-ray background at a level known sources cannot explain, which has been read as a sign of early accreting black holes. Retest it on new data: the cross-power and coherence of source-subtracted Euclid Q1 near-infrared maps with eRASS1 0.5-2 keV maps at 5-60 arcmin, with intervals.

Data: Euclid Quick Release Q1 VIS and NISP images over the Euclid Deep Field North/South and Fornax fields (public), eRASS1 0.5-2 keV images and exposure maps (eROSITA-DE DR1), Gaia and Euclid catalogues for source masking.

Method: fixed before computing: the fields, masks (sources above a fixed depth, a fixed radius law), the multipole bins, the estimator (cross-power with the mask's mode coupling corrected, coherence C = P_xy^2/(P_xx P_yy)). Errors from rotated and jackknifed maps; nulls: cross-power of the NIR with randomly rotated X-ray maps; the stellar and zodiacal residuals by their own cross-correlations.

Expected precision: limited by the Q1 area (~60 deg^2); the cross-power at the level claimed from Spitzer x Chandra is detectable at 3-5 sigma if present.
