## Survivors, ranked

1. **Row 7: ZTF DR2 SN peculiar-velocity fσ8.** The forecast was published and not carried out. The data and validation sims are public. The release's own "not yet suited for parameter inference" caveat is the main risk, and so is a ZTF DR2.5 scoop.
2. **Row 10: DESI DR1 void size function S8.** The catalogues are public and the VSF has been measured but not fitted. The payoff is modest.
3. **Row 9: spectroscopic DESI supervoid ISW.** This is cheap. It is mostly a null-closing test of an anomaly that is fading.
4. Rows 6 and 11 carry high scoop risk from collaborations. Row 11 also needs the KiDS-Legacy shape catalogue confirmed public.
