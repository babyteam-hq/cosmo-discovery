# Results (R-122, cosmo-deep-2, 2026-11-01)

R-121's confrontation (the eRASS1 weak-lensing-calibrated mass scale against CMB lensing) redone on the ACT DR6 lensing
release, with what its three referees required built in. Every number from `erass1_act.py` (usage in its docstring; it imports
R-121's `erass1_cmblens.py` for the catalogue, the halo model and the fits); data under `data/` (gitignored). This file is the
worker's own. Logs in `logs/` (gitignored).

**Fixed before the first fit (2026-11-01 03:45 UTC, after the footprint counts and before any map was stacked at the clusters):**
the statistic is the one-halo amplitude A_1h with the two-halo amplitude A_2h free, and the mass rescaling b_M (M_true = b_M
M_eRASS1, the profiles rebuilt, A_2h profiled out) with its 68 % and 95 % intervals (Δχ² < 1, < 3.84); the samples are the
primary catalogue in the footprint, M500 < 4 × 10^14, the cosmology sample, the cosmology sample below 4 × 10^14, each **outside
the release's SZ-cluster mask** (the 6' holes inpainted in the CMB maps before the reconstruction, which the footprint counts
below showed hold 26 % of the clusters; the clusters inside them are a separate, diagnostic sample); the null-mean correction is
the mask-matched random stack on the data map; the covariance is from the simulations. Every amplitude computed is in the logs
and counted under "trials" in the finding.
