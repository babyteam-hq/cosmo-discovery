# Results (R-131, cosmo-deep-11, 2026-11-01)

The stacked thermal Sunyaev-Zeldovich signal of the eRASS1 clusters below the SZ catalogues' mass limit, against their
weak-lensing-calibrated masses: the Y_500-M500 relation at 1-8 x 10^14 M_sun on the Planck 2015 MILCA/NILC and ACT DR6
Compton-y maps. The open part of R-121's candidate A (`results-R121.md` row A, verdict "partly novel"). Every number from
`erass1_ysz.py` (usage in its docstring; it imports `erass1_cmblens.py` for the catalogue); data under `data/` (gitignored);
logs under `logs/` (gitignored). This file is the worker's own.

## Literature check (2026-11-01, 30 min, before any download beyond the catalogues)

Searched (extended): "eRASS1 stacked Compton-y", "eROSITA clusters tSZ stacking Planck ACT", "Y-M relation eRASS1 weak
lensing masses", "eROSITA Compton stacking Y_SZ 2026", "stacked thermal SZ X-ray selected clusters Planck y map eROSITA".
Nothing stacks a Compton-y map on the eRASS1 catalogue for a Y-M relation. What exists, and is the comparison set:
Aymerich et al. 2025 (arXiv:2509.02068; PSZ2 Chandra hydrostatic masses vs DES shear, 1-b = 0.844 +0.055/-0.062, and a
cluster-by-cluster consistency with the eRASS1 masses of the shared clusters), Aymerich et al. 2025b (arXiv:2509.08673;
Planck counts under the eROSITA cosmology need 1-b = 0.63 +- 0.03), the ACT DR6 cluster catalogue (arXiv:2507.21459;
<M_eRASS1> = 0.99 +- 0.01 <M_ACT> for the shared clusters, all above the ACT limit), SPT-Deep (arXiv:2503.17271; median mass
ratio 0.96 +- 0.05 on 18 matches), Stiskalek & Desmond 2026 (arXiv:2601.15935; digital-twin halo masses scored on the Planck y
map around clusters and validated against the eROSITA WL-calibrated masses, which they find higher than tSZ masses), the
eRASS1 X-ray scaling relations (arXiv:2511.14356, Y_X not Y_SZ), and the old stacks of X-ray-selected clusters on Planck
(Planck early XI 2011, MCXC with L_X masses). The stacked SZ Y-M relation of the eRASS1 clusters with the eRASS1 masses, two
decades below the SZ catalogues, is open. Proceeding.

## Fixed before any stack (2026-11-01 09:30 UTC, after the catalogues were read and before any y map was opened at a cluster)

- **Samples.** Primary: the eRASS1 cosmology sample (5,259; its M500, R500, z from the primary catalogue by DETUID), restricted
  to 0.1 < z < 0.8 and 1 x 10^14 <= M500 < 8 x 10^14 M_sun, inside each map's footprint. Supplementary: every primary-catalogue
  cluster with PCONT < 0.5 in the same mass and redshift range. Informative rows only: z < 0.1, M500 < 10^14, M500 >= 8 x 10^14.
  Footprint: the map's own mask (Planck: the NILC release's point-source and Galactic mask; ACT: the wide GAL070 mask, with
  the inpainted-region and subtracted-source masks applied) clean within 15' of the cluster and >= 80 % clean within 1 deg.
  Clusters inside the ACT release's cluster mask (SNR > 6 SZ detections) are kept in the primary stack only if the ACT y map
  is not inpainted there (the inpainted-regions mask decides); the SZ-detected ones are an informative row.
- **Bins.** Five mass bins, edges 1, 1.4, 2, 2.8, 4, 8 x 10^14; two redshift bins 0.1-0.3 and 0.3-0.8; the headline table is the
  five mass bins over 0.1-0.8, the 5 x 2 cells are the redshift test.
- **Aperture and statistic.** For each cluster the Arnaud et al. 2010 universal pressure profile (UPP) at the cluster's R500
  (from its catalogue M500 and z in the eRASS1 cosmology, H0 = 67.77, Om = 0.29), projected to a y profile, truncated at 5 R500,
  normalised to the spherical Y_500 the reference relation predicts for the cluster's mass, convolved with the map beam
  (Planck: 10' FWHM Gaussian; ACT: the release's beam file) and the pixel window, averaged over the cluster's lognormal mass
  posterior (R-122's nodes: the catalogue's M500_L/H as sigma_lnM, mass and R500 moving together). The stack is the masked mean of
  the map in annuli around the X-ray positions (Planck: 0-2.5, 2.5-5, 5-7.5, 7.5-10, 10-15, 15-20, 20-30, 30-40, 40-60';
  ACT: 0-1, 1-2, 2-3, 3-4, 4-6, 6-8, 8-10, 10-15, 15-20, 20-30'); the statistic per bin is the GLS amplitude A of the bin-averaged
  template with a free constant (the local background), so A = Y_500(observed) / Y_500(reference) at the bin's masses, and the
  bin's Y_500 is A times the posterior-averaged reference prediction at the bin's mean mass. Check: the fixed aperture, y
  within 10' minus the 20-40' annulus (Planck) and within 5' minus 10-20' (ACT), converted to Y_500 by the same template.
- **Model.** Y_500 E(z)^-2/3 D_A^2 [Mpc^2] = 10^A (M500 / 3 x 10^14)^B, A and B free, fitted to the five bins with their
  covariance (and to the ten cells, with E(z)^2/3 self-similar evolution assumed). Reference relation for the templates and for
  the mass-scale ratio: Planck 2013 XX (arXiv:1303.5080), E^-2/3 D_A^2 Y_500 / 1e-4 Mpc^2 = 10^-0.19 [(1-b) M / 6e14]^1.79 with
  1-b = 0.8 (so the prediction at an eRASS1 mass M is the relation at the hydrostatic mass 0.8 M). Implied mass-scale ratio:
  M_true = b_M M_eRASS1 gives A_ratio = ((1-b) b_M / 0.8)^1.79, i.e. b_M = 0.8 A_ratio^(1/1.79) / (1-b), quoted at 1-b = 0.8 and
  over 0.7-0.9. Second comparison: Arnaud et al. 2010's REXCESS relation (arXiv:0910.1234 eq. 25-26, XMM hydrostatic masses:
  E^-2/3 D_A^2 Y_500 = 2.925e-5 x I(1) (M / 3e14 h70^-1)^1.78 h70^-1 Mpc^2 with I(1) = 0.6145, and I(5)/I(1) = 1.796 for the
  cylindrical 5 R500 aperture, which the template code reproduces to 0.001) and the ACT/SPT calibrations as published.
- **Nulls and covariance.** 200 rotated-map stacks per map (the map and its mask turned together in longitude by 25-335 deg,
  the clusters fixed, the footprint selection re-applied) give each bin's annulus covariance (Hartlap corrected) and the null
  distribution of its amplitude; 20,000 mask-matched random positions give the null-mean profile subtracted from the data;
  the catalogue's own neighbours: the model y map of every catalogue cluster (its template at its reference Y_500) stacked at
  the same positions with the same annuli, minus each cluster's own template, is the predicted neighbour contamination and
  is subtracted from the data with its amplitude tied to the fitted A.
- **Foregrounds and corrections.** The two Planck maps against each other; the ACT CIB-deprojected map (cib 1.7, 10.7 K)
  against the baseline; the relativistic SZ correction stated for the bins' temperatures (the catalogue's kT), not applied.
- **The claim** is the table: per bin Y_500 with its interval, A and B with intervals per map, the implied b_M with its
  interval and the prior it rests on. No morphology split. Every amplitude computed is logged and counted under trials.
