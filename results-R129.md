# Results (R-129, cosmo-deep-9, 2026-11-01)

A contamination budget for the ACT DR6 (and Planck PR3) CMB-lensing maps from templates that cannot carry lensing signal
(Milky Way stars, Galactic dust and HI, imaging depth, PSF size, the Gaia and unWISE scan patterns), propagated into the published
κ × galaxy amplitudes with the DES statistic (Omori et al. 2019 arXiv:1810.02342 Sec. V; Chang et al. 2022 arXiv:2203.12440 App.
B1: the bias on C^{κg} from a systematic S is C^{κS} C^{gS} / C^{SS}). Every number from `kappa_templates.py` (usage in its
docstring); data under `data/` (gitignored); logs in `logs/` (gitignored). This file is the worker's own. The trajectory check
("partly novel"; the open part is this test on ACT DR6 / Planck with Gaia and Legacy templates, propagated into unWISE, DESI LRG
and Quaia) is in the brief and in `LOG.md`; not rerun.

**Fixed before the first cross-spectrum (2026-11-01 06:30 UTC, after the data were fetched and inspected and before any κ map was
multiplied by any template):**

- **Templates (24), each standardised to zero mean and unit variance over the analysis footprint and upgraded to nside 512.**
  Gaia DR3 source counts per nside-256 pixel (ESA archive TAP, `gaia_healpix_index(8)`), six: G 12-16, 16-18, 18-20, 20-21,
  and 16-20 split at BP−RP = 1 (blue, red). Legacy Surveys DR9 imaging properties (the DESI DR1 LSS `LRG_mapprops_healpix_nested_
  nside256_{N,S}` maps, the systematics the DESI LRG papers weight against), eleven: STARDENS, EBV, HI, PSFDEPTH_G, PSFDEPTH_R,
  PSFDEPTH_Z, PSFDEPTH_W1, GALDEPTH_Z, PSFSIZE_G, PSFSIZE_R, PSFSIZE_Z. The Quaia selection-function templates as released (Storey-
  Fisher et al. 2024, Zenodo 10403370, nside 64), seven: dust, stars, m10, mcs, unwise, unwisescan, mcsunwise. Excluded on purpose:
  KAPPA_PLANCK (signal), CALIB_* (0.6 % coverage), the EBV_MPF/SGF14/CHIANG variants and HALPHA (duplicates of EBV at this
  precision). KiDS-1000 and DES Y3 survey-property maps: not fetched (time; the Legacy maps cover the DESI samples).
- **Maps.** ACT DR6 lensing v1: baseline (the statistic), tonly, polonly, cibdeproj, galcut040 (each with its release mask,
  apodized, degraded to nside 512; the κ alm band-limited to L ≤ 1200). Planck PR3 MV (`COM_Lensing_4096_R3.00`, the klm rotated
  from Galactic to equatorial, its mask likewise).
- **Band powers.** Pseudo-C_L on the common weight W = (ACT mask, apodized) × (template footprint) [× (galaxy mask) for the
  propagation], the same W on every field of a ratio, binned in 8 bands with edges 20, 50, 100, 150, 200, 300, 400, 600, 800,
  decoupled with the binned MASTER matrix computed from W's power spectrum (closed-form Wigner 3j, m = 0; NaMaster could not
  be built in this container). The two top bands are inside the nside-256 templates' pixel window; they are kept because the
  propagation uses the same pixelised template on both sides.
- **Latitude bands.** The whole footprint; |b| < 30°; 30° ≤ |b| < 50°; |b| ≥ 50° (the pixel's Galactic latitude at nside 512,
  applied as a binary cut on W).
- **Null.** Baseline map: each template crossed with each of the release's 400 simulated reconstructions through the identical
  pipeline (W, bands, decoupling), per latitude band; plus 90 rotations of the data alm in right ascension (4° k, k = 1..90, the
  mask turned with the map, the template fixed) as a check. The other ACT variants and Planck: the 90 rotations (their own
  simulations are not streamed; the brief's budget).
- **Pass criterion** (per template × map × latitude band): χ² of the 8 decoupled band powers against the null covariance
  (Hartlap-corrected), PTE = the fraction of nulls with χ² ≥ the data's. **Contamination "detected"** when PTE < 1/400 (no
  simulation exceeds the data) on the whole footprint of the baseline map, and the same template has PTE < 0.01 in at least one
  of tonly, polonly or galcut040 (so it is not one map variant's noise). "Marginal": PTE < 0.01 on any band. 24 templates × 4
  latitude bands = 96 tests per map, so about one marginal result per map is expected by chance; the count of marginal results
  against that expectation is reported as such. Everything else is an upper limit: the 95 % one-sided limit on each band power
  is the data value plus 1.64 null spreads, and the propagated limit is as below.
- **Propagation** (samples: DESI Legacy Imaging LRGs in the four photometric-redshift bins of Zhou et al. 2023 as released with
  White et al. 2022 (Zenodo 5834378, `lrg_s0N_del/msk.hpx0256`), the sample ACT DR6 × LRG (Sailer et al. arXiv:2407.04607, Kim et
  al. arXiv:2407.04606) and Planck × LRG (White et al. arXiv:2111.09898) are measured on; Quaia G < 20.5 (Alonso et al.
  arXiv:2306.17748; Piccirilli et al. 2024 on ACT), δ_g = n / (n̄ s) − 1 with the released selection function s, footprint s > 0.5.
  unWISE: no public galaxy map was found on Zenodo or NERSC (LAMBDA answers 403 from this cloud), so it is not propagated; said
  so.) For each sample g and template S: X_S(b) = C^{κS}(b) C^{gS}(b) / C^{SS}(b) per band (decoupled, common W). The bias on
  the κ × g amplitude: ΔA_S / A = Σ_b X_S(b) C^{κg}(b) / σ_b² ÷ Σ_b C^{κg}(b)² / σ_b², with C^{κg}(b) the measured cross band
  powers on the same W and σ_b their spread over the 400 simulations crossed with g; σ_A / A = (Σ_b C^{κg}(b)² / σ_b²)^{-1/2}.
  Reported per template: ΔA_S / A, ΔA_S / σ_A, and the null spread of ΔA_S over the 400 simulations (κ_sim in C^{κS}; the 95 %
  limit is |ΔA_S| + 1.64 spreads). Total per sample: the quadrature sum over the 24 templates (which double-counts the correlated
  ones, Gaia bins and depth bands, so it is an upper bound) and the largest single template.
- **The claim is the table.** Per template and map: the whole-footprint PTE and the sign of the low-L band powers; per sample:
  ΔA_S / A and ΔA_S / σ_A with the null spread, the quadrature total and the maximum. No sentence about S8 beyond what the table
  says. Every statistic computed is in the logs and counted under "trials" in the finding.

**Deviations from the fixed plan, decided while preparing the inputs and before the first κ × template spectrum (06:40 UTC):**
HI is dropped (the DESI DR1 LSS maps that carry it cover only the DR1 spectroscopic footprint, 2,300 deg² of ACT's; the Legacy
templates come instead from the DR9 `pixweight-1-dark` file, the whole imaging footprint, 9,100 deg² of ACT's, which has no HI
column), so 10 Legacy templates. The Quaia `mcs` and `mcsunwise` templates are the Magellanic-Cloud masks, zero everywhere in the
ACT footprint: constant, not testable, so 5 Quaia templates; **21 templates in all**. The White et al. LRG maps are in Galactic
coordinates (a third of their footprint would sit at |b| < 15° read as equatorial, none does read as Galactic) and are resampled
to equatorial pixels. The alm and the coupling matrix run to L = 1100 (not the top band edge, 800): with the matrix cut at 850
the recovery test on Gaussian maps with a known spectrum through the ACT × Legacy weight came back 9-19 % high in the two top
bands and 1-5 % elsewhere; the full-sky identity test passes to 1e-13. **The Quaia `unwise` template is the unWISE source-density
map** (Storey-Fisher et al. 2024's selection-function feature), which is mostly galaxies at high latitude and so carries real
lensing signal: it is not a zero-signal template. Decided on reading its definition, after the data band powers were printed
(07:05 UTC) and before any PTE: it stays in the tables as a check that the pipeline sees a known κ × galaxy signal, and is left
out of the contamination totals.
