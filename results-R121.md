# Results (R-121, cosmo-deep-1, 2026-11-01)

One cross-community confrontation to paper depth (`deep-1.md`: the candidates, their detectability estimates and the
trajectory verdicts). Every number from a script in this directory with its usage line in the docstring; data under `data/`
(gitignored). This file is the worker's own.

| candidate | trajectory check (verbatim verdict line, context-free Opus, 2026-11-01) |
|---|---|
| A: eRASS1 WL-calibrated masses vs the Planck Compton-y map (Y_SZ-M500 across the whole catalogue) | VERDICT: partly novel: the eRASS1 WL mass scale vs Planck SZ (hydrostatic-bias-corrected, 1−b≈0.84) masses is already compared cluster-by-cluster for PSZ2–eRASS1 matches in Aymerich et al. arXiv:2509.02068 (and vs ACT DR6, ⟨M_eRASS1⟩=0.99⟨M_ACT⟩, arXiv:2507.21459); a stacked Planck/ACT Compton-y Y_SZ–M500 relation over the full eRASS1 sample, including sub-SZ-detection masses and its mass/z/selection dependence, remains open |
| B: eRASS1 WL-calibrated masses vs Planck PR3 CMB lensing (tSZ-deprojected κ stack) | VERDICT: novel: no prior work answers this question |
| C: eRASS1 masses vs DESI DR1 BGS velocity dispersions | VERDICT: partly novel: arXiv:2411.09735 (Li+2024) already shows for 22 eRASS1 clusters that kinematic (≈hydrostatic) masses fall well below the eRASS1 WL-calibrated masses (WL ~110% higher), and arXiv:2402.08453 / arXiv:2609.31852 already measure eRASS1 dispersions and eRASS1×DESI DR1 dynamics; what remains open is a direct, large-sample (~10^3 clusters) stacked σ_v–M500 test of the eRASS1 WL-calibrated mass scale with DESI DR1 spectroscopy |
| D: Planck y map vs the DESI DR1 BGS group catalogue | VERDICT: partly novel: the stacked Planck matched-filter Y_SZ–halo-mass relation for spectroscopic groups at 1e12–1e14.5 is answered by Lim et al. 2018 (arXiv:1710.06856, 2MRS/6dF/SDSS groups), and for DESI DR9 groups by arXiv:2201.12591; what remains open is testing the specific DESI DR1 BGS group catalogue's halo masses against tSZ, which no published work has done |

Chosen: B (the one "novel"); fallback A narrowed to its open part. Killed on data before any referee: DESI DR1 peculiar
velocities vs the 2M++ field (no DR1 PV value-added catalogue exists).
